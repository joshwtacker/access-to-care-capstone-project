
Access to Care: Opioid Overdose Risk vs Treatment Availability in the United States
Author: "Josh Tacker"
---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between opioid overdose mortality, socioeconomic factors, and treatment facility availability across U.S. counties.

The goal is to identify geographic disparities in access to care and highlight areas where treatment resources may not align with overdose risk.

---

## Research Questions

1. How do socioeconomic factors correlate with opioid overdose mortality rates?
2. Are treatment facilities distributed in proportion to overdose mortality rates?
3. Does treatment access correlate with lower overdose mortality?
4. Which counties show the largest gaps between overdose risk and treatment availability?

---

## Data Sources

- CDC WONDER (Opioid overdose mortality data)
- U.S. Census Bureau (ACS socioeconomic data via API)
- SAMHSA (Treatment facility locations)
